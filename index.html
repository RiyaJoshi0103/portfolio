Featured work · Igniters Hub

Backpack AI

An offline clinical assistant for community health workers in places with poor or no internet. A guided 8-step flow takes a visit from voice intake to diagnosis and an EMR report, and the app checks prescriptions for risky drug combinations.

Voice to notesFFmpeg audio processing, DeepFilterNet noise removal and Whisper speech-to-text, 5+ languages.
Hybrid LLMOllama runs locally; Groq is used when online.
RAG answersChromaDB retrieval so answers come from medical documents.
Medicine conflict alertsFlags drug–drug interactions from the DDInter database, with severity and a recommended action. The clinician can review or override.
Guided visit flowVoice intake → registration → consultation → AI questions → diagnosis → EMR report.
Works offlineShipped as a one-click Windows installer; switches between offline and online mode.
How I stopped it inventing readings Shipping it as an installer
